About

Engineering experience grounded in sustainability practice

My background combines environmental engineering research with several years of professional experience in sustainability, environmental auditing, quality assurance, certification, and responsible manufacturing. I have assessed environmental and social performance across manufacturing and supply-chain facilities and have worked with international standards covering environmental management, occupational health and safety, quality, recycled materials, organic textiles, traceability, and responsible sourcing.

My academic work focuses on practical environmental technologies and decision tools, including sustainable water treatment, resource-efficient manufacturing, life cycle assessment, circular production systems, and data-driven environmental monitoring.

Research

Research areas and proposed work

My research interests focus on scalable environmental solutions with practical relevance for U.S. industries and communities.

01

Water & Wastewater Treatment

Developing and evaluating sustainable approaches for purification, industrial effluent treatment, monitoring, and water reuse.

02

Life Cycle Assessment

Applying LCA to compare environmental impacts, identify hotspots, and support better material and process decisions, including recycled textile systems.

03

Smart Environmental Monitoring

Exploring sensor-enabled and AI-assisted systems for real-time environmental monitoring, performance optimization, and data-informed decision-making.

04

Circular Economy & Resource Efficiency

Advancing practical methods to reduce waste, improve resource productivity, increase recycled content, and support circular manufacturing models.

05

Industrial Sustainability

Connecting environmental management systems, energy and water efficiency, traceability, and supplier performance to measurable sustainability outcomes.

06

Accessible Tools for SMEs

Designing simplified environmental assessment and decision-support approaches that can be adopted by small and medium-sized enterprises.
