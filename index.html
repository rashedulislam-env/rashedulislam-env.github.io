About Me

A career connecting engineering, sustainability assurance, research, and education.

My professional path is interdisciplinary by design.

I began in textile engineering, where I developed a practical understanding of manufacturing systems, material performance, quality, supply chains, and industrial operations. I then expanded into Environmental Sciences & Management and Environmental Engineering, building expertise in environmental management systems, wastewater and water systems, life cycle thinking, GIS, sustainability, and environmental decision-making.

From 2019 to 2024, I progressed through auditing and quality-assurance roles in international sustainability and certification work. My responsibilities included third-party assessments, management-system evaluation, traceability and chain-of-custody verification, nonconformity review, corrective-action verification, auditor feedback, and QA oversight across manufacturing supply chains.

At Lamar University, I served as a Research Assistant with the Center for Resiliency and completed an M.S. in Environmental Engineering in 2026. My research portfolio spans AI-assisted water purification, coastal carbon and water-quality modeling, GIS-integrated environmental site assessment, blue-carbon remote sensing, smart-city air-quality monitoring, and materials research.

In 2026, I added secondary science education to this pathway as an Environmental Systems Teacher with Port Arthur ISD, bringing professional environmental practice into the classroom and strengthening my ability to communicate complex scientific concepts to diverse audiences.

My long-term objective is to develop and communicate practical environmental solutions that improve water security, resource efficiency, industrial sustainability, climate resilience, and evidence-based environmental decision-making.
